About the book

An Indian tailwind

Before the British arrived, the Indian subcontinent produced almost a quarter of the world’s economic output. By Independence, its share had fallen below four percent. Today India is the world’s most populous nation and one of its fastest-growing major economies.

The Emerging Indian Century explains how India is set to become one of the world’s leading economic powers in the twenty-first century. It traces the arc from Nehru’s tryst with destiny, through the Licence Raj and the reforms of 1991, to the digital public infrastructure, GST and new institutions of the Modi era.

Each chapter pairs a thread of Indian history and culture with the story of a company shaping the country’s future. Wimbledon’s towels lead back to Gandhi’s spinning wheel and on to Welspun. Temple gold leads to Titan and Kalyan. A cola war leads to Varun Beverages. Drawing on first-hand conversations with founders and leaders, Wesley Richards offers an outsider’s view of an economy entering a multi-decade period of compounding growth.

About the author

Wesley Richards

  • CFA Charterholder
  • Asian fund manager
  • Barrister, Middle Temple
  • LSE

Wesley Richards is an Asian fund manager with a focus on India. He has travelled extensively across the country and has met many of its leading founders, promoters and CEOs.

Wesley has worked for a UK single family office for ten years, the last five as its in-house Asian fund manager. In 2009 he founded Frontier Asset, providing investment consultancy and research services.

Before moving into wealth management, he worked as a pastor for a non-denominational church. A former international rower, he served as an elected local councillor for the Royal Borough of Windsor & Maidenhead from 2015 to 2019 and is a parent-founder of an independent school in Windsor.

He holds a degree in Economics from the University of London and a Masters in Environment and Development from the London School of Economics. He was called to the Bar by the Middle Temple in 2005 and is a CFA Charterholder. He is married with four sons.
